**AUTONOMOUS AI ENTERPRISE** providing a **decentralized** alternative to traditional **management** through a **locally** hosted **swarm** of frontier-class **intelligence**.

This architecture delivers **executive-grade** execution and **strategic** oversight with **unprecedented** operational efficiency, reclaiming **thousands** of **annual** work-hours while **ensuring total** data sovereignty within a **secure** and compliant **framework**. 

The [office](framework/office.md) is staffed by [management](framework/management.md) — CEO, CTO, CFO, CAO — directing [specialists](framework/specialists.md) who route strategy into action, and a [worker swarm](framework/workers.md) of nine lightweight agents spawned on demand and torn down on completion.<br/>

Agents carry composable [traits](traits/README.md) and [influences](traits/influence/README.md) — including [agnostic](traits/agnostic.md), enabling model switching mid-task with preserved context.<br/>

The full model stack — text, code, vision, image, video, music, voice, 3D, science, and more — is catalogued in [research/models.md](research/models/models.md). The case for running it all locally is in [research/local-ai.md](research/models/local-ai.md). Context discipline — lean system prompts, spawn-fresh workers, and heartbeat compression — is [what makes 24/7 autonomous operation an architectural guarantee](research/architecture/context-discipline.md#why-this-is-the-condition-for-247-autonomous-operation).<br/>
